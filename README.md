# Relic Helper

Relic Helper helps you track crafting materials and plan combinations for Mythic Relic I or Mythic Relic II.

## What it does

- Tracks quantities of all listed materials.
- Lets you choose which Mythic Relic you want to craft.
- Calculates a crafting plan from the quantities in your inventory.
- Shows the combining actions and the materials remaining after the plan.

## How to use

1. Open Relic Helper.
2. In **Inventory**, enter how many of each material you own.
3. In **Calculation Mode**, select **Mythic Relic I** or **Mythic Relic II**.
4. Tap **Combine** in **Final Outputs** to calculate a plan.
5. Review **Combining Actions** to see what to craft.
6. Review **Remaining Inventory** to see which materials are left.
7. Tap **Clear Quantities** to reset all inventory quantities to zero.
